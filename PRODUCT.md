# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Students are the primary users. Parents and teachers support students' learning and progress.

## Product Purpose

ZarifÇözüm is an online mathematics learning platform. Its success measure has not yet been defined.

## Positioning

Not yet determined.

## Operating Context

The product is a Turkish-language web platform with student, parent, and teacher roles.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The current project includes mathematics lessons and practice, assessments, study planning and progress tracking, live lessons, guided question hints, teacher assignments, and parent progress reporting.
- The current site describes this version as a demo and says that the examples do not mean every listed grade or feature is complete.
- The intended overall product range is grades 5–12. For 2026–27, current project copy identifies grades 5–7 and 9–11 as following the Maarif Model; grades 8 and 12 follow the previous curriculum. The working example is for grade 6.
- The product's differentiating position and success measure are undecided.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is ZarifÇözüm. The existing project uses Turkish-language interface copy and includes brand marks in `assets/brand-mark.png` and `assets/brand-mark-white.png`. No additional voice or identity constraints have been confirmed.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains the current web implementation, mathematics learning flows, and brand-mark assets. The site's package prices and package details are explicitly presented as demo placeholders, not as current commercial offers. No customer testimonials, organization details, or other external proof have been confirmed.

## Product Principles

- Keep students' learning and progress primary; parents and teachers are supporting users.
